About this report
This sustainability report covers the reporting period 1 January to 31 December 2025. We publish a sustainability report every year together with our annual report.

Our sustainability strategy is approved by the Board of Directors and is reviewed annually by the Executive Committee.

Energy Management
Energy use in our machining plants is our most material environmental impact.
We aim to reduce energy consumption per unit produced by 30% by 2030 compared with a 2020 baseline.
The energy target is owned by our Chief Operating Officer and progress is reviewed quarterly by the Executive Committee.
In 2024 we installed 1.2 MW of rooftop solar panels at our Boras plant and replaced legacy air compressors to achieve our energy target.
In 2025 we completed energy audits at all three production sites as part of our energy efficiency roadmap.

Employee Engagement, Diversity and Inclusion
Our goal is to reach 40% women in management positions by 2027.
In 2025 we launched a mentoring programme for female engineers and organised university recruitment events in line with our diversity strategy.
Women in management: 2023: 28% | 2024: 31% | 2025: 34%
Our annual employee engagement survey had a response rate of 86% in 2025.
Labour Practices
Our Labour Rights Policy commits us to fair wages, safe working hours and freedom of association at all sites.
In 2024 we conducted labour rights audits at 12 supplier sites.
Working conditions for our employees significantly improved during the year.
---PAGE---
Data Security
We maintain an information security policy approved by the Board of Directors, which sets out our approach to data protection and cybersecurity.
In 2025 we completed ISO 27001 certification of the information security management system at our headquarters.
Product Design and Lifecycle Management
Our strategy is to become a circular business by designing products for durability, repair and recycling.
